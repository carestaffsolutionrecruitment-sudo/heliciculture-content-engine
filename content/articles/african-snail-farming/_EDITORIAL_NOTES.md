## Fact-Check Before Publishing

Figures are given as ranges drawn from general heliciculture extension literature. The items below are the most time-sensitive or jurisdiction-specific, and should be confirmed with a primary source or local expert before going live:

1. **Kenya:** Current KWS licensing category and procedure for snail farming under the Wildlife Conservation and Management Act 2013.
2. **Uganda:** Whether UWA wildlife-use rights apply to snail farming, and the current procedure.
3. **South Africa:** The current NEM:BA AIS list status of *Lissachatina fulica*, *Archachatina marginata* and *Achatina achatina*, and provincial permit requirements for *L. immaculata* and *Cornu aspersum*.
4. **Ghana and Cameroon:** Current responsibilities of MoFA and the Wildlife Division (Ghana), and of MINEPIA and MINFOF (Cameroon).
5. **The United States:** That the prohibition on live GALS imports is still current (it is cited as an example in articles 02 and 10).
6. The species biology ranges in article 01 (clutch sizes, maturity ages). Cross-check against at least one peer-reviewed source per species.
7. The climate figures for named towns in articles 05 and 07. Spot-check against national meteorological normals.

## Deliberate Omissions

- **No fixed prices** in any currency. The articles describe how prices move over the year instead, because volatile exchange rates (NGN, GHS, KES) would quickly make quoted prices wrong. A dated "price snapshot" sidebar per country can be added if there is a maintenance process to keep it current.
- **No named individual experts, farms or brands**, to avoid unverified claims.
