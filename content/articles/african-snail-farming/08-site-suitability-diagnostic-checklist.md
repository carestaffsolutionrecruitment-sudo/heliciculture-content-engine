# Site Suitability Diagnostic Checklist for Giant African Land Snail Farms

> **Part 8 of the series:** *[Snail Farming Across African Regions](./00-series-hub.md)*

Use this checklist **before** you build pens or buy breeding stock. It turns the limits described in [Climatic Limitations](./07-climatic-limitations.md) into a field test you can score. It takes about **one month** to complete properly, because the most important measurements have to be taken **during your driest season**.

---

## What You Will Need

| Tool | Purpose | Approximate cost class |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| Digital thermo-hygrometer, ideally a **data logger** | Records temperature and RH at snail level | Low |
| Min/max thermometer | Records the coldest night and hottest day | Low |
| Soil pH test kit or meter | Measures soil acidity | Low |
| Spade and a 30 cm ruler | Soil profile and drainage tests | — |
| Bucket and stopwatch (or phone) | Drainage test | — |
| Notebook or spreadsheet | Records results | — |
| Access to local weather records | Long-term rainfall and temperature | Free or low |

---

## Part A: Hard Stop Checks

**Answer these first.** A single "Yes" means **do not proceed with outdoor GALS farming on this site** until it is resolved.

| # | Question | Yes / No |
|---|----------|----------|
| A1 | Is the planned species **prohibited or unlicensed** for your location (alien-species law, wildlife law)? | ☐ |
| A2 | Has the land been affected by **oil spills, mining, industrial waste or dumping**? | ☐ |
| A3 | Has the land been sprayed with **pesticides or herbicides** in the last 12 months, or is it next to heavily sprayed fields? | ☐ |
| A4 | Does the site experience **frost** in any normal year? | ☐ |
| A5 | Is the site **regularly flooded** during the rainy season? | ☐ |
| A6 | Is the site in a **desert or Sahelian zone** (annual rainfall under about 600 mm)? | ☐ |

> If you answered "Yes" to A4 or A6, GALS farming is only possible in a **fully enclosed, climate-controlled house**. See [Mitigation Systems §4](./09-mitigation-systems.md#4-indoor-climate-controlled-housing). If you answered "Yes" to A1, A2 or A3, **find another site or species**.

---

## Part B: Scored Climate Assessment

Score each item on the scale given, then add up the section total. Use **long-term records** for rainfall and season length, and **your own pen-level measurements** for humidity and temperature.

### B1. Rainfall and dry season (maximum 20 points)

| Item | 5 points | 3 points | 1 point | 0 points | Score |
|------|----------|----------|---------|----------|-------|
| Mean annual rainfall | More than 1,800 mm | 1,300–1,800 mm | 1,000–1,299 mm | Under 1,000 mm | |
| Length of dry season (months with under 50 mm rain) | 0–2 | 3 | 4 | 5+ | |
| Rainfall pattern | Two rainy seasons a year (bimodal) or rain all year | Long single rainy season (7+ months) | Single rainy season of 5–6 months | Single rainy season of 4 months or less | |
| Harmattan or dry-wind exposure | None or negligible | Mild (a few weeks) | Moderate (1–2 months) | Severe (3+ months) | |

### B2. Relative humidity, measured in the pen during the driest month (maximum 15 points)

| Item | 5 points | 3 points | 1 point | 0 points | Score |
|------|----------|----------|---------|----------|-------|
| Night-time average RH (18:00–06:00) | Above 85 % | 75–85 % | 65–74 % | Below 65 % | |
| Daytime minimum RH (typical day) | Above 65 % | 50–65 % | 35–49 % | Below 35 % | |
| Days per month with daytime RH below 50 % | 0–3 | 4–10 | 11–20 | More than 20 | |

### B3. Temperature (maximum 15 points)

| Item | 5 points | 3 points | 1 point | 0 points | Score |
|------|----------|----------|---------|----------|-------|
| Coolest-month mean night minimum | Above 20 °C | 17–20 °C | 13–16 °C | 12 °C or below | |
| Hottest-month mean day maximum (in the shade) | 31 °C or below | 32–33 °C | 34–35 °C | Above 35 °C | |
| Elevation (tropical Africa) | Below 1,000 m | 1,000–1,400 m | 1,401–1,800 m | Above 1,800 m | |

**Climate subtotal (B1 + B2 + B3):** ____ / 50

---

## Part C: Soil Assessment

### C1. Field tests (maximum 20 points)

| Item | 5 points | 3 points | 1 point | 0 points | Score |
|------|----------|----------|---------|----------|-------|
| Soil pH (average of 3+ samples) | 6.5–7.5 | 6.0–6.4 or 7.6–8.0 | 5.5–5.9 or 8.1–8.5 | Below 5.5 or above 8.5 | |
| Squeeze test | Holds shape, crumbles when pressed (loam) | Slightly gritty or slightly sticky | Very sandy or very sticky | Pure sand or heavy clay | |
| Drainage (30 cm hole) | Drains in 30 minutes to 2 hours | 10–30 minutes or 2–4 hours | Under 10 minutes or 4–8 hours | More than 8 hours (waterlogged) | |
| Topsoil depth | More than 30 cm | 20–30 cm | 10–19 cm | Under 10 cm, or laterite or hardpan | |

**Soil subtotal:** ____ / 20

> **Note:** A poor soil score can usually be fixed by **importing good topsoil into hutch boxes or trench pens**. Count soil as a cost item, not a deal-breaker, unless it is contaminated (Part A).

---

## Part D: Micro-Climate and Site Factors (maximum 15 points)

| Item | 3 points | 2 points | 1 point | 0 points | Score |
|------|----------|----------|---------|----------|-------|
| Existing shade (tree canopy, plantain or banana stands) | Dense natural shade | Partial shade | Little shade | Full sun | |
| Wind exposure | Sheltered | Light breeze | Exposed for part of the year | Ridge-top or open plain | |
| Reliable water within 50 m | Year-round, clean | Year-round, needs treatment | Seasonal | None | |
| Predator and ant pressure | Low | Moderate | High | Very high (active soldier-ant colonies on site) | |
| Security (theft risk) | Fenced and supervised | Fenced only | Partly fenced | Open and unsupervised | |

**Site subtotal:** ____ / 15

## Part E: Market Access (not scored, but required)

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Distance to the nearest major snail market (km) | |
| Number of buyers spoken to in person | |
| Have any buyers stated volume, size grade and price in writing? | ☐ Yes ☐ No |
| What is the dry-season price compared with the rainy-season price, per grade? | |
| Is there a processing or cold-chain option (for meat or mucin)? | ☐ Yes ☐ No |

## Interpreting Your Score

**Total = Climate (50) + Soil (20) + Site (15) = ____ / 85**

| Total score | Rating | Recommendation |
|-------------|--------|----------------|
| **70–85** | ✅ **Excellent** | Suitable for outdoor or semi-outdoor GALS farming. *A. achatina* is viable if the B2 score is 12 or more. |
| **55–69** | 🟢 **Good** | Suitable for *A. marginata* with standard shading, mulch and dry-season irrigation |
| **40–54** | 🟡 **Marginal** | Viable only with **covered housing and moisture-retention systems**. Pilot with a small herd for 12 months before scaling. |
| **25–39** | 🟠 **Poor** | Requires **enclosed, climate-controlled housing**. Check the business case carefully against energy and water costs. |
| **Under 25** | 🔴 **Unsuitable** | Do not farm GALS here. Consider buying from producers in suitable regions, processing or trading instead, or a cool-climate species if permitted. |

### Important override rules

- If **B2 (humidity) scores 5 or less**, treat the site as **no better than "Marginal"**, whatever the total.
- If the **coolest-month night minimum scores 0** (12 °C or below), or **B3 scores 5 or less**, treat the site as **no better than "Poor"**, whatever the total.
- **Any "Yes" in Part A** overrides the score entirely.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Forest-belt farm near Benin City, Nigeria

| Section | Score | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| B1 Rainfall | 14 / 20 | About 2,000 mm; dry season about 3 months; long single rainy season; mild Harmattan |
| B2 Humidity | 11 / 15 | Night-time RH about 85 % in the driest month; daytime minimum about 50 % |
| B3 Temperature | 13 / 15 | Low elevation; warm nights; hot-season maximum about 33 °C |
| C Soil | 16 / 20 | pH 6.2; loamy; good drainage |
| D Site | 11 / 15 | Plantain shade; borehole; soldier ants present |
| **Total** | **65 / 85** | 🟢 **Good.** *A. marginata* with dry-season irrigation. |

### Example 2: Peri-urban plot near Abuja (FCT), Nigeria

| Section | Score | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| B1 Rainfall | 6 / 20 | About 1,300 mm; dry season about 5 months; strong Harmattan |
| B2 Humidity | 0 / 15 | Night-time RH about 55 % in January; daytime minimum about 20 %; more than 20 dry days |
| B3 Temperature | 7 / 15 | About 480 m elevation; Harmattan night minimum about 15 °C; March maximum about 35 °C |
| C Soil | 10 / 20 | Lateritic patches; pH 5.8 |
| D Site | 8 / 15 | Little shade; borehole available |
| **Total** | **31 / 85** | 🟠 **Poor.** The humidity override also applies. **Enclosed housing with humidification is needed.** |

### Example 3: Smallholding near Nairobi, Kenya (about 1,750 m)

| Section | Score | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| B1 Rainfall | 10 / 20 | About 900 mm; 5+ dry months; two rainy seasons; no Harmattan |
| B2 Humidity | 5 / 15 | Night-time RH about 80 %; daytime minimum about 40 % in the dry months |
| B3 Temperature | 6 / 15 | July nights about 11 °C (0 points); mild days; elevation 1,401–1,800 m |
| C Soil | 12 / 20 | Red volcanic soil; pH 5.6 |
| D Site | 10 / 15 | Fenced; piped water |
| **Total** | **43 / 85** | The score alone says "Marginal", but **both overrides apply** (humidity and cold nights), so the rating is 🟠 **Poor.** **Insulated, heated, humidified housing is needed.** |
