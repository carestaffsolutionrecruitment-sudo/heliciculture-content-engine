# SOP M3: Daily Feeding and Hygiene Round

| SOP No. | Version | Effective date | Review date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-SOP-M3 | 1.0 | [date] | [date + 12 months] | Farm Manager |

## 1. Purpose
To feed all stock a balanced, calcium-rich ration at the right time and amount, and to remove spoiled feed and waste before it ferments and causes disease.

## 2. Scope
All production, breeding and nursery units. Quarantine units follow the same routine, with dedicated equipment, done **last**.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Duty operator:** morning and evening rounds.
- **Farm manager:** sets rations, approves feed formulas (Module 3 calculator) and checks the feed store weekly.

## 4. Safety Warnings
> ⚠️ **Never add salt** to feed, water or supplements. Salt is toxic to snails.
>
> ⚠️ **Mould and mycotoxins:** discard any damp, caked or mouldy feed. Never feed it, even partly.
>
> ⚠️ **Hygiene:** wear gloves; wash your hands between zones; work from clean units (nursery, breeding) to dirty (growers, quarantine). Never take quarantine equipment into other units.
>
> ⚠️ **Raw cassava leaves** and pesticide-treated forage must not be fed.

## 5. Procedure

### Morning round: clean-out
5.1 **Remove all uneaten meal, wilted or spoiled forage, droppings and dead snails** from every unit. Bag dead snails separately for approved disposal (SOP M4).

5.2 **Assess the residue** from the previous evening's feed, and record it as none, small or large. Adjust today's ration: none → +10 %; small → no change; large → −10 %.

5.3 **Scrub the feeding tiles or troughs:**
- **Daily:** scrape and rinse them with clean water.
- **Weekly:** wash with a food-safe detergent, rinse thoroughly, and dry before reuse. Rotate in a clean set while one set dries.

5.4 **Refresh the water stations:** empty, rinse and refill the shallow dishes, or re-wet the sponges, with clean, dechlorinated water. Hatchling units use saturated sponges only, to prevent drowning.

5.5 **Record** the mortality count per unit (Module 4 Log 1) and the residue score.

### Afternoon / evening round: feeding (late afternoon or at dusk, when snails become active)
5.6 **Prepare the meal ration:** weigh the day's ration for each unit (Module 3 calculator, Tab 3, adjusted daily meal). Use the oldest compliant batch first, and record the feed batch number.

5.7 **Prepare the fresh botanicals:** wash the forage in clean water, inspect it for mould, rot and pests, and chop it for hatchlings. Examples:
- ***Cornu*:** lucerne, clover, chard, cabbage, carrot tops.
- ***Achatina*:** pawpaw, banana and cocoyam leaves, ripe pawpaw.

5.8 **Calcium:**
- Check that the meal formula includes calcium (10–13 % DM for *Cornu* growers).
- In nursery and breeding units, top up the **free-choice calcium dish** (crushed limestone or oyster shell) or replace worn cuttlebone.

5.9 **Place the feed** on clean tiles or troughs, never scattered on the substrate: meal on tiles, forage alongside.

5.10 **Mist or water** as required by SOP M2.

### Weekly (farm manager)
5.11 **Inspect the feed store:**
- Bins sealed, off the ground and rodent-proof.
- No damp or mould.
- Stock rotated, with batch numbers and dates recorded.

5.12 **Review residue trends,** and adjust rations or formulas if units are consistently over- or under-fed.

### Fortnightly and every 3–4 weeks
5.13 Sample-weigh 30–50 snails per batch and update the growth tracker (Module 3, Tab 5).

5.14 Grade by size and rebalance densities to the stage maximums (Module 3, Tab 4).

## 6. Accountability Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Signed by | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Clean-out complete; mortality and residue recorded | Duty operator | Daily (AM) |
| Rations weighed and fed; feed batch recorded | Duty operator | Daily (PM) |
| Feeding tiles and troughs deep-cleaned | Duty operator | Weekly |
| Feed store inspection | Farm manager | Weekly |
| Growth sample and grading | Farm manager | Fortnightly / monthly |

## 7. Records
Daily Feeding Log (unit, ration kg, residue score, feed batch, initials); Feed Store Log; Growth Tracker. Keep them for at least 2 years.

## 8. Deviations
Record any missed feed, any feed batch found mouldy (quarantine the batch and inform the supplier), and any unit with a large residue for 3 days in a row (investigate climate or disease).
