# SOP M1: Stock Health Assessment on Arrival

| SOP No. | Version | Effective date | Review date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-SOP-M1 | 1.0 | [date] | [date + 12 months] | Farm Manager |

## 1. Purpose
To make sure that only healthy, correctly identified and legally sourced snails enter the farm, and that new stock is isolated until it is shown to be disease-free.

## 2. Scope
All incoming breeding stock, juveniles and growers, from any supplier, including transfers between your own sites.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Receiving operator:** carries out steps 5.1–5.9 and completes the arrival form.
- **Farm manager:** approves acceptance or rejection (5.10) and signs the release from quarantine.

## 4. Safety Warnings
> ⚠️ **Zoonotic risk:** wear nitrile gloves when handling all incoming snails, especially *Achatina*-group species, which can carry rat lungworm (*Angiostrongylus cantonensis*). Wash your hands after handling. Never eat, drink or touch your face during intake.
>
> ⚠️ **Legal:** do not accept species you are not legally permitted to keep (e.g. giant African land snails in the US, or non-native species without authorisation). Do not release or dispose of rejected live stock into the environment.
>
> ⚠️ **No salt or chemicals** on live snails at any stage.

## 5. Procedure

### Before arrival
5.1 Confirm the quarantine room is cleaned, disinfected, rinsed and dry, with dedicated tools, boxes, footwear and clothing, and the climate set to species targets (*Cornu* 18–22 °C / 75–90 % RH; *Achatina* 24–28 °C / 80–95 % RH).

### On arrival
5.2 **Check documents:** species, supplier, origin (captive-bred or wild), quantity, dates, and any health, movement or import certificates. **If any are missing or the species is wrong, stop** and quarantine the consignment unopened while the manager decides.

5.3 **Move straight to quarantine.** Never open consignments in production areas.

5.4 **Check the transport conditions:** container type, ventilation and temperature on arrival. Record any signs of overheating, chilling or crushing.

5.5 **Inspect a sample** of at least 10 % of the snails (all of them if fewer than 50):
- **Shell integrity:** cracks, chips, holes, thinning, pitting.
- **Hydration:** body plump and moist (good) or shrunken, dry and deeply retracted (dehydrated). A thick epiphragm is normal after cool transport.
- **Response:** retracts when gently touched (good), or slow or no response (poor).
- **Mucus and smell:** clear mucus and no foul odour (good), or discoloured mucus and a foul smell (infection or death).
- **Parasites:** check near the breathing pore with a hand lens for moving white specks (mites).
- **Species identity:** check shell shape and colour against reference photographs.

5.6 **Count the dead and moribund snails** in the whole consignment, and record the percentage.

5.7 **Weigh** 30 snails individually (or all, if fewer). Record the mean, minimum and maximum.

### Post-transport rehydration and settling
5.8 **Rehydrate gently:**
- Lightly mist the snails and the box surfaces with clean, dechlorinated water at room temperature. **Never submerge snails.**
- Provide moist substrate and a saturated sponge or very shallow water dish.
- **Dormant *Cornu*:** allow them to warm gradually to 15–20 °C over several hours. Do not move them straight from cold into heat.
- **Achatina:** keep them warm (24–28 °C) and humid.
- Offer fresh washed greens after 12–24 hours, and formulated meal once the snails are active and feeding.

5.9 **Record the 24-hour follow-up:** the number now active, any further deaths, and any abnormal signs.

### Decision
5.10 The farm manager decides:
- **Accept into quarantine:** dead or moribund under ~5 %, no disease signs, documents complete.
- **Accept with a treatment plan:** minor issues, such as dehydration, which resolve with rehydration.
- **Reject:** wrong or illegal species, signs of infectious disease, high mortality or missing documents. Notify the supplier. Dispose of rejected or dead stock only through an approved animal by-product route, never into the environment.

5.11 Enter the batch in the **Quarantine Register** (Module 4 Log 2). Minimum quarantine is **4–6 weeks**, with daily checks under SOP M4.

## 7. Records
Stock Arrival Assessment form; Quarantine Register; supplier documents. Keep them for at least 2 years.

## 8. Deviations
Any deviation (e.g. arrival without documents, quarantine space unavailable) must be recorded, with the reason and the manager's decision.
