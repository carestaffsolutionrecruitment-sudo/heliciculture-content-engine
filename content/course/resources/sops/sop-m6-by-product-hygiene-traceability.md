# SOP M6: By-Product Hygiene and Traceability

| SOP No. | Version | Effective date | Review date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-SOP-M6 | 1.0 | [date] | [date + 12 months] | Farm Manager / QA Lead |

## 1. Purpose
To collect and process snail mucin and eggs (caviar) hygienically, keep them completely separate from meat processing, and make every by-product batch traceable to its source.

## 2. Scope
Mucin (snail secretion filtrate), snail caviar, and processed shells.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **By-product operator:** collection, vessel sterilisation, processing and batch-tagging.
- **QA lead:** process validation records, testing, batch release and register audits.

## 4. Safety Warnings
> ⚠️ **Different legal regimes:** mucin falls under cosmetics law, caviar under food law, and shells under animal by-product rules. Never relabel or redirect a by-product to a different use without the correct approvals.
>
> ⚠️ **Cross-contamination:** meat processing is a source of bacteria and allergens. By-product work must be separated **in space or time**, never in parallel on the same surfaces.
>
> ⚠️ **Animal welfare:** mucin collection must be non-lethal and gentle. No salt, acid or irritants, and follow the collection-interval limits.
>
> ⚠️ **Sterilisation hazards:** autoclaves, boiling water and hot-air ovens can cause burns. Follow the equipment instructions and wear heat-resistant gloves.

## 5. Procedure

### A. Separation from meat processing
5.1 Run by-product work in a **dedicated room**. Or, in the shared processing room, run it **before** any meat processing that day, after a full clean and sanitise.

5.2 Use **dedicated, colour-coded equipment** for by-products (e.g. blue for mucin, green for caviar). It must never be used for meat.

5.3 Wear clean clothing: a fresh gown or apron, hairnet and new gloves. No staff member moves from meat processing to by-product work without a full change and handwash.

### B. Sterilising collection vessels and tools
5.4 Wash the vessels, sieves, spatulas and jars with detergent and rinse them.

5.5 Sterilise them by a validated method, e.g. autoclave, boiling for at least 10 minutes, or hot-air oven (for glass). Record the method, time and temperature.

5.6 Store sterilised items covered, in a clean cabinet. Use them within the validated storage time, or re-sterilise.

### C. Mucin collection and processing
5.7 Check the collection group (healthy, purged, hydrated; interval since last collection OK). Record its ID.

5.8 Collect using the approved non-lethal method into sterile vessels. Keep each vessel covered between uses.

5.9 Filter (coarse → fine → microfiltration per the validated specification), add the stabiliser or preservative at the specified dose, and record each parameter (Module 6 QA Sheet A).

5.10 Return the snails to normal husbandry, and record their condition after collection.

### D. Egg collection and caviar processing
5.11 Collect clutches from the laying pots **within 24 hours of laying**. Record the pot and breeder unit IDs.

5.12 Wash gently in potable water; sort and reject broken, cloudy, discoloured or developing eggs.

5.13 Brine, pack into sterilised jars and pasteurise **exactly per the validated recipe and process**. Record the salt concentration, times and temperatures (Module 6 QA Sheet B).

5.14 Chill to ≤ 5 °C straight away, and log the storage temperature.

### E. Shells
5.15 Collect the shells from meat processing into a separate, labelled container. Wash, heat-treat by the approved method, dry, crush and grade (Module 6 QA Sheet C). Never feed them back to snails.

### F. Batch-tagging and traceability
5.16 **Assign a Batch ID** at collection: `MU-`, `CV-` or `SH-` + YYMMDD + sequence.

5.17 **Tag every container** at the point of collection, not later, with the Batch ID, product, date, operator initials and status (`IN PROCESS` / `HOLD – AWAITING TESTS` / `RELEASED`).

5.18 Record the **source batch link** (snail batch, breeder unit or meat processing batch) in the By-Product Batch Register.

5.19 Keep products on **HOLD** until the test results and the QA review are complete. Only the QA lead changes the status to RELEASED.

5.20 On dispatch, record the customer, quantity and Batch IDs on the invoice and in the register.

## 6. Accountability Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Signed by | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Room or slot cleaned and separated from meat processing | By-product operator | Each session |
| Vessels sterilised (method, time, temperature recorded) | By-product operator | Each load |
| Batch ID tagged at collection; source link recorded | By-product operator | Each batch |
| Process parameters within the validated limits | QA lead | Each batch |
| Test results reviewed; batch released | QA lead | Each batch |
| Register audit: every released batch traceable both ways | QA lead | Monthly |

## 7. Records
Module 6 QA Sheets A–C; By-Product Batch Register; sterilisation log; lab certificates; welfare log for mucin collection groups. Keep them for at least 2 years, or longer if the law or customer contracts require it.

## 8. Deviations
Record any batch processed outside the validated limits, any untagged container or any cross-contamination event. Place the affected batches on HOLD pending the QA decision.
