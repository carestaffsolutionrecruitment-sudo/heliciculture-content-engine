# SOP M5: Harvest, Purging and Processing Safety Protocol

| SOP No. | Version | Effective date | Review date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-SOP-M5 | 1.0 | [date] | [date + 12 months] | Farm Manager / Food Safety Lead |

## 1. Purpose
To harvest, purge, condition, dispatch and process snails humanely and hygienically, producing safe, traceable meat that meets food hygiene law.

## 2. Scope
All snails harvested for sale as food, from pre-harvest selection to packed, chilled or frozen product.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Harvest operator:** selection, intake inspection, purging and conditioning (5.1–5.10).
- **Processing operator:** dispatch, extraction, evisceration, cleaning and packing (5.11–5.22).
- **Food safety lead / farm manager:** verifies the critical control points (section 6) and releases each batch.

## 4. Safety Warnings
> ⚠️ **Scald and burn risk:** boiling water and steam. Wear heat-resistant gloves and an apron, and use baskets or slotted ladles; never reach into boiling water. Keep the floor dry, and keep children and untrained staff out of the processing room.
>
> ⚠️ **Knives and sharp tools:** use cut-resistant gloves on the non-knife hand.
>
> ⚠️ **Food safety:** snails must always be **cooked thoroughly** before eating. *Achatina* can carry rat lungworm, so wear gloves for all raw handling.
>
> ⚠️ **Animal welfare:** no salt, vinegar or detergent on live snails; no cold-water starts; no slow freezing of live snails; no cracking or extracting live *Achatina*.
>
> ⚠️ **Allergen:** molluscs. Keep them separate from other foods, and label them where the law requires (UK, EU and many other markets).

## 5. Procedure

### A. Pre-harvest selection
5.1 Sample-weigh 30–50 snails from the unit. Harvest if **≥ 80–90 %** meet the buyer's weight specification **and** have a fully formed, hard shell lip.

5.2 Collect the snails into clean, ventilated containers. Return any undersized or soft-lipped snails to the unit.

5.3 Open the **Batch Record** (Module 5 downloadable) and assign the Batch ID.

### B. Intake inspection
5.4 Reject cracked, foul-smelling, non-retracting or dead snails. Record the count and the rejection rate.

### C. Purging fast (snails active; no feed)
5.5 Place the snails in clean, perforated, ventilated purge containers, in a single layer where possible.

5.6 **Purge conditions:**

| | *Cornu aspersum* | *Achatina* group |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | **48–72 hours** | **24–72 hours** |
| Temperature | ~15–20 °C | ~22–26 °C |
| Humidity | Mist 1–2× daily with clean water | Mist 1–2× daily with clean water |

5.7 **Rinse the containers daily** to remove faeces. Record the start and end times, temperatures and any deaths.

### D. Cold conditioning (CCP 1: species-specific)
5.8 After purging, chill to induce dormancy:

| | *Cornu aspersum* | *Achatina* group |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | **2–6 °C** | **8–12 °C**. ⚠️ **Never 2–6 °C** |
| Duration | 24–48 hours | Short chill, several hours |

5.9 **Record the chiller temperature** at the start, midpoint and end. If it goes out of range, correct it and record the action. If *Achatina* were exposed to 2–6 °C, inform the manager.

5.10 Confirm dormancy: *Cornu* sealed with an epiphragm or inactive; *Achatina* inactive.

### E. Humane dispatch (CCP 2)
5.11 Prepare the processing room: clean and sanitise all surfaces and tools, put on clean protective clothing, and keep the raw and finished-product areas separate.

5.12 Bring a large volume of water (**≥ 10× the weight of the snails**) to a **full rolling boil**. Prepare an ice-water bath.

5.13 Add the snails **in small batches**, so the water returns to the boil almost immediately.

5.14 Boil: *Cornu* **3–5 minutes**; *Achatina* **5–8 minutes**, depending on size.

5.15 Transfer them straight into the ice bath, and record the batch times.

### F. Hygienic extraction and hepatopancreas removal (CCP 3)
5.16 Working over clean stainless steel, draw out each body with a snail fork, following the spiral. Keep the meat on ice.

5.17 **Remove the visceral mass**, including the hepatopancreas (the dark, coiled *tortillon*), at its junction with the foot. For *Achatina*, slit the foot and remove the intestine and any remaining dark tissue.

5.18 **Inspect every piece.** No viscera may remain. The supervisor initials the batch record.

5.19 Keep the shells separate if they will be used for by-products (SOP M6).

### G. Cleaning, preservation and packing
5.20 **Remove the mucin** (dispatched meat only):
- ***Cornu*:** coarse salt and a little vinegar.
- ***Achatina*:** lime juice or food-grade alum, with flour or cornmeal.

Massage, rinse under cold running water, and repeat until the water runs clear.

5.21 **Preserve** using the validated method:
- **Chill** to 0–5 °C.
- **Freeze** to −18 °C or colder.
- **Cook in court-bouillon**, then cool to below 8 °C within 90 minutes.
- **Pasteurise** per the validated process.

5.22 **Pack and label:** product name, species, net weight, Batch ID, packing date, use-by date, storage temperature, producer details and the **mollusc allergen**. Record the final processed net weight and the meat yield %.

## 6. Critical Control Points and Accountability Checkpoints

| CCP / checkpoint | Critical limit | Monitoring | Corrective action | Signed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCP 1: Cold conditioning | *Cornu* 2–6 °C; *Achatina* 8–12 °C | Temperature at start, mid, end | Adjust; extend; inform manager | Harvest operator |
| CCP 2: Dispatch | Full rolling boil; timed per species | Visual boil check; timer | Wait for boil; re-boil the batch | Processing operator |
| CCP 3: Evisceration | No visceral tissue remaining | Inspect every piece | Rework; reject | Supervisor |
| CCP 4: Cooling and storage | < 8 °C within 90 min; storage 0–5 °C or ≤ −18 °C | Probe and fridge/freezer log | Discard if limits are breached and safety can't be assured | Processing operator |
| Batch release | Batch Record complete | Review | Hold until complete | Food safety lead |

## 7. Records
Harvest and Processing Batch Record; Cold Storage Temperature Log (Module 4 Log 4); cleaning records. Keep them for at least 2 years.

## 8. Deviations
Record any CCP failure, with the product affected, the decision (rework, hold or discard) and the root cause.
