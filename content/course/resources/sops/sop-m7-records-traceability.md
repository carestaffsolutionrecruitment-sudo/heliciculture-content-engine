# SOP M7: Records and Traceability

| SOP No. | Version | Effective date | Review date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-SOP-M7 | 1.0 | [date] | [date + 12 months] | Farm Manager |

## 1. Purpose
To keep complete, accurate and retrievable records, so that every batch of snails, feed lot, product and sale can be traced. This supports food safety compliance, regulatory inspections, customer audits, insurance claims and internal management.

> **Regulator note:** in England, food safety on a snail farm is enforced by the **local authority** under Food Standards Agency (FSA) guidance. Other countries have their own food safety and veterinary authorities. The Care Quality Commission (CQC) regulates health and social care, not food production, so it does not apply here.

## 2. Scope
All records created under SOPs M1–M6 and M8, plus purchases, sales, staff training and compliance documents.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **All staff:** complete the records for their tasks before the end of each shift.
- **Records lead** (named by the farm manager): weekly digital archiving, backups and the quarterly traceability test.
- **Farm manager:** monthly audit and the annual compliance review.

## 4. Safety and Data Warnings
> ⚠️ **Integrity:** records must be completed at the time of the task, never back-filled from memory. Corrections are made by striking through the original (it must stay legible), then initialling and dating the change. Never erase or overwrite.
>
> ⚠️ **Personal data:** customer and staff records contain personal data. Store and share them in line with data protection law (e.g. UK GDPR): restricted access, secure storage, and retention limits.
>
> ⚠️ **Backups:** a single copy is not a record system. Keep at least two copies, one of them off-site or in the cloud.

## 5. Procedure

### A. Batch identity (the backbone of traceability)
5.1 Assign IDs at the point of origin, and carry them on every subsequent record:

| Item | ID format | Assigned at |
|---|---|---|
| Stock purchase / hatch batch | `ST-YYMMDD-seq` | Arrival (SOP M1) or hatch |
| Feed lot | Supplier lot no., or `FD-YYMMDD-seq` for home-mixed feed | Receipt or mixing |
| Harvest / processing batch | `[farm]-[species]-YYMMDD-seq` | Harvest (SOP M5) |
| By-product batch | `MU-` / `CV-` / `SH-YYMMDD-seq` | Collection (SOP M6) |
| Sales invoice | Invoice no. | Sale |

### B. Record set

| Area | Records | Source SOP |
|---|---|---|
| Stock | Arrival assessments, quarantine register, hatch records, unit transfers | M1, M4 |
| Environment | Daily micro-climate log, logger downloads, pH and calibration logs | M2 |
| Feed | Feed receipts (supplier, lot, date), mixing records, feeding log, feed store checks | M3 |
| Health | Mortality register, isolation, cleaning and disinfection, disposal, vet visits, outbreak reports | M4 |
| Processing | Harvest and processing batch records, CCP monitoring, cold storage temperatures | M5 |
| By-products | QA sheets, batch register, lab certificates | M6 |
| Sales and dispatch | Invoices (with Batch IDs), delivery notes, dispatch and receipt temperatures, complaints | M8 |
| Compliance | Registrations, permits, licences, insurance, HACCP plan, SOP versions | — |
| People | Training records for each SOP, competency sign-offs | All |

### C. Daily workflow
5.2 Complete the paper or digital logs during each task.

5.3 Before the end of the shift, check that every log for the day is complete and initialled.

5.4 Put paper records in the day's folder or tray for scanning.

### D. Weekly digital archiving (records lead)
5.5 Scan the paper records to PDF. Name the files `YYYY-MM-DD_[log type]_[unit or batch].pdf`.

5.6 File them in the archive structure: `/Records/[Year]/[Area]/[Month]/`.

5.7 Export the spreadsheet logs (climate, mortality, feed, batch register) to read-only copies (PDF or CSV) in the same structure.

5.8 **Back up** to a second location (cloud storage or an off-site drive), and check that the backup completed.

5.9 File the paper originals in a dry, secure cabinet.

### E. Linking records (traceability chain)
5.10 Each harvest batch record lists its **source stock batch, units and feed lots** used in the last cycle.

5.11 Each invoice and delivery note lists the **Batch IDs** supplied.

5.12 Each by-product batch lists its **source batch**.

### F. Quarterly traceability test (records lead)
5.13 Pick one sold batch at random. Within **4 hours**, trace it:
- **back** to its source stock, units, feed lots, climate and mortality logs, and processing CCP records;
- **forward** to every customer and the quantity each received.

5.14 Record the time taken, the gaps found and the corrective actions. **Pass** = complete in under 4 hours.

### G. Retention and audit
5.15 Keep records for at least **2 years**, or longer if the law, a permit or a customer contract requires it.

5.16 **Monthly audit** (farm manager): sample the logs for completeness, accuracy and signatures, and record the findings.

5.17 **Annual compliance review:** check that registrations, permits, licences, insurance, the HACCP plan and SOP versions are current, and note any regulatory changes.

## 6. Accountability Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Signed by | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Daily logs complete before end of shift | Each operator | Daily |
| Scan, archive and backup verified | Records lead | Weekly |
| Monthly records audit | Farm manager | Monthly |
| Traceability test passed (< 4 h) | Records lead → farm manager | Quarterly |
| Annual compliance review | Farm manager | Annually |

## 7. Records
Archive index; backup log; traceability test reports; audit reports.

## 8. Deviations
Record any missing record, failed backup or failed traceability test, with the root cause and corrective action. Re-test within 30 days after a failure.
