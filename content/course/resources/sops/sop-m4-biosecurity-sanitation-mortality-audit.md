# SOP M4: Biosecurity Sanitation and Mortality Audit

| SOP No. | Version | Effective date | Review date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-SOP-M4 | 1.0 | [date] | [date + 12 months] | Farm Manager |

## 1. Purpose
To stop disease from entering and spreading on the farm by controlling hygiene, isolating sick stock, disposing of dead snails safely and auditing mortality.

## 2. Scope
All staff, visitors, units, equipment and vehicles on site.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **All staff:** hygiene rules (5.1–5.6).
- **Duty operator:** daily mortality removal and recording, isolation of sick stock, footbath changes.
- **Farm manager:** weekly and monthly audits, outbreak decisions, disposal arrangements and contacts with the veterinary adviser.

## 4. Safety Warnings
> ⚠️ **Disinfectants:** use only products approved for food-animal premises, at the label dilution and contact time. Wear gloves and eye protection when mixing. Never mix products (e.g. chlorine with acids or ammonia). Store them locked, away from feed.
>
> ⚠️ **Chemical residues kill snails.** Rinse every disinfected surface thoroughly with clean water and let it dry fully before restocking.
>
> ⚠️ **Zoonotic risk:** wear gloves when handling dead or sick snails, especially *Achatina*. Wash your hands afterwards.

## 5. Procedure

### Entry hygiene
5.1 **Zoning:** work in the order clean (nursery, breeding) → production (growers) → dirty (dispatch, waste) → quarantine **last**. Never go back from dirty to clean without a full clean and change.

5.2 **Handwashing stations** at every zone entrance (soap, water, paper towels). Wash on entry and exit.

5.3 **Footbaths / boot dips** at zone entrances:
- Brush visible soil off boots **before** dipping, because organic matter inactivates disinfectant.
- Stand in the dip for the contact time on the label.
- Change the solution **daily**, or sooner if it is visibly soiled, and record the change.

5.4 **Glove replacement rules.** Change gloves:
- between zones;
- after handling dead or sick snails;
- after handling waste;
- whenever they are torn or contaminated;
- at least every 2 hours of continuous work.

5.5 **Dedicated clothing and footwear** for each zone, with separate sets for quarantine.

5.6 **Visitors:** sign the visitor log, wear overshoes and coveralls, are escorted, and do not enter breeding rooms unless essential.

### Daily mortality audit
5.7 **Remove all dead snails** from every unit during the morning round, wearing gloves. Do not leave dead snails in a unit overnight.

5.8 **Count and record** the dead and culled snails per unit in Module 4 Log 1. Note the clinical sign codes (MR, SP, ST, EM, FO, RF, LE, EP, MI).

5.9 **Check the status flag** (Normal / Alert / Outbreak). Escalate Alert or Outbreak to the manager the same day.

### Isolation of sick stock
5.10 Move snails showing signs of disease (retraction failure, foul smell, discoloured mucus, mantle recession, mites) into a labelled **isolation box** in the quarantine room, with dedicated tools.

5.11 Check isolated snails daily. Those that recover may return to their unit after **14 days** without signs, with the manager's approval. Humanely dispatch moribund snails (rolling boil, SOP M5) and record them as culled.

### Safe disposal of dead snails
5.12 **Bag them** in sealed, labelled bags. Store them in a closed, pest-proof container (chilled or frozen if collection is not daily).

5.13 **Dispose of them through an approved animal by-product route** (e.g. a licensed collector or approved incineration). Record the date, quantity and consignment note.

5.14 **Never** compost dead snails on site, bury them in growing areas, feed them to animals or put them in general waste without authorisation.

### Between-batch sanitation
5.15 Empty each unit, and remove and replace or heat-treat the substrate.

5.16 **Clean:** scrape, then wash with detergent and water to remove all organic matter.

5.17 **Disinfect** with an approved product at the label dilution, for the full contact time.

5.18 **Rinse thoroughly** with clean water, **dry completely**, then restock. Record each step in Module 4 Log 3.

### Outbreak response (Outbreak status)
5.19 Stop all movement of snails, equipment and staff from the affected unit.

5.20 Remove all dead and moribund snails, and record them.

5.21 Correct the climate, residues and density (SOPs M2 and M3).

5.22 Contact the veterinary adviser, and send samples to a diagnostic laboratory where available.

5.23 Keep the unit isolated until mortality returns to baseline for at least **2 weeks**. Then clean and disinfect it (5.15–5.18).

5.24 Write up the root cause and lessons learned.

## 6. Accountability Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Signed by | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Footbaths changed; handwash stations stocked | Duty operator | Daily |
| Mortality removed, recorded and flagged | Duty operator | Daily |
| Disposal recorded with consignment note | Duty operator | Each collection |
| 7-day mortality trend reviewed for all units | Farm manager | Weekly |
| Between-batch sanitation verified before restock | Farm manager | Each batch |
| Logs audited for completeness | Farm manager | Monthly |

## 7. Records
Module 4 Logs 1–5 (mortality, quarantine and isolation, cleaning and disinfection, cold storage temperatures, disposal); visitor log; outbreak reports. Keep them for at least 2 years.

## 8. Deviations
Record any breach of zoning, missed footbath change, delayed disposal or unapproved product use, with the corrective action.
