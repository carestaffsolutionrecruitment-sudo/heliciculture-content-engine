**Notices**

- **Figures are indicative.** Temperatures, densities, feed targets and thresholds are evidence-based starting points. Calibrate them using your own records, laboratory analysis and veterinary advice.
- **Validation.** Thermal processes, curing recipes, mucin processing and shelf lives must be validated by a qualified food technologist or cosmetic laboratory before sale.
- **Regulation varies by country** and changes over time. These documents name the frameworks to check, but they are not legal advice.
