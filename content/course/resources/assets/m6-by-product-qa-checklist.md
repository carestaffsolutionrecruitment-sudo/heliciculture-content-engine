# Module 6 Downloadable: By-Product Processing and Quality Assurance Checklist

**Commercial Heliculture Masterclass · Snail World (snailworld.org)**
**Format:** three single-page QA sheets (mucin, caviar, shells) plus a by-product batch register. Each by-product batch gets its own ID that links back to the source snail batch.

> **Regulatory reminder:** mucin is a **cosmetic ingredient** (cosmetics law). Caviar is a **food** (food law; check any novel food status). Shells are **animal by-products** (by-product rules apply to feed uses). Processes and limits must be validated by a qualified food technologist or cosmetic laboratory before sale.
