# Part B: Wholesale Pricing Guide

## 1. Product Grading Standards

Agree final grade limits with each buyer and attach them to the contract.

**Cornu aspersum** (live weight per snail)

| Grade | Petit-gris | Gros-gris | Shell and condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | ≥ 10 g | ≥ 20 g | Fully formed hard lip; no cracks or chips; clean; uniform size within the grade |
| **B** | 8–10 g | 15–20 g | Formed lip; minor cosmetic marks; no cracks |
| **C** | 7–8 g | < 15 g, or any grade with cosmetic shell flaws | Sold for processing only (clean meat, prepared products) |

**Achatina group** (*A. marginata*, *A. achatina*; live weight per snail)

| Grade | Live weight | Shell and condition |
|---|---|---|
| **A (jumbo)** | ≥ 150 g | Thick, intact lip; no cracks; active and retracts quickly |
| **B (large)** | 100–150 g | Intact; firm lip |
| **C (medium)** | 80–100 g | Intact; for processing or price-sensitive channels |

**Processed meat grades:** graded by piece size or count per kg, colour and absence of viscera or shell fragments.

## 2. Price Build-Up

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Full cost per kg (by product form) | `=(production + processing + packaging + delivery + overhead allocation) ÷ saleable kg` (from the Module 7 model) |
| Grade differential | Grade A = base × (1 + premium %); Grade C = base × (1 − discount %). Set the percentages from buyer willingness to pay and your yield mix |
| Minimum wholesale price | `=full cost per kg × (1 + target margin %)` |
| Channel check | `buyer's resale price × (1 − buyer's required margin %) ≥ your minimum wholesale price` |

## 3. Volume Pricing Tiers (template)

Offer discounts only where larger orders genuinely lower your unit cost through fewer deliveries, less packing time and better batch planning.

| Tier | Monthly volume (kg or units) | Price | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| List | Below tier 2 threshold | List price | Standard terms |
| Tier 2 | ≥ [threshold] | List × (1 − [x] %) | Fixed delivery day |
| Tier 3 | ≥ [threshold] | List × (1 − [y] %) | 3-month agreement, fixed delivery schedule |
| Contract | Agreed annual volume | Negotiated; price review clause | 12-month supply agreement; forecast shared monthly |

**Rule:** check every tier price stays at or above the minimum wholesale price. Review prices quarterly against feed, energy and transport costs.

## 4. Cold-Chain Logistics Guarantee (standard terms)

| Product | Dispatch and delivery temperature | Packaging | Monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live *Cornu* (dormant) | Cool, ~4–10 °C; ventilated | Mesh bags or ventilated crates in insulated boxes | Temperature recorded at dispatch and delivery |
| Live *Achatina* | Moderate, ~18–26 °C; humid and ventilated; **never chilled** | Ventilated, escape-proof crates | As above |
| Chilled meat or caviar | 0–5 °C | Sealed packs in insulated boxes with ice packs | Data logger or indicator on routes over 2 hours |
| Frozen meat | −18 °C or colder | Vacuum packs, insulated boxes, gel packs or dry ice | Data logger; frozen vehicle for long routes |

**Guarantee:**

- Product arrives within the stated temperature range and transit time.
- The buyer checks and records the temperature on receipt and reports any issue within **[24] hours** with a photo and reading.
- Out-of-specification deliveries are **replaced or credited in full** (unless caused by the buyer's own handling).
- Repeated failures trigger a joint logistics review.

**Other legal requirements:** live snails can only be supplied where their sale is legal (no live giant African land snails where prohibited). Any export needs the importing country's certificates.
