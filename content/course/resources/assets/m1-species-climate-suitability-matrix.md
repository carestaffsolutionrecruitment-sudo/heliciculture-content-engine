# Module 1 Downloadable: Species Comparison and Climate Suitability Matrix

**Commercial Heliculture Masterclass · Snail World (snailworld.org)**
**Format:** 2-page PDF (A4 landscape). Page 1: comparison matrix. Page 2: site scoring worksheet and monthly climate check.

> **Use:** complete this before buying stock or building housing. Legal status is a pass/fail gate: a species that is not legal where you farm is excluded, whatever its score.
