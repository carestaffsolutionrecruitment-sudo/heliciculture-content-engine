# Commercial Heliculture Masterclass: AI Image Prompts

**Snail World (snailworld.org) · ProductDyno Product ID 44288**

There is one hero image per module. Each comes in two versions:

- **Midjourney:** a single line with parameters.
- **Nano Banana** (Gemini image generation): written as a natural-language brief. Nano Banana does not use `--` parameters, so the aspect ratio and exclusions are stated in words.

## Rules for Every Image

1. **No text in the image.** AI generators garble lettering, gauge readouts, labels and document text. Add titles and captions afterwards in the layout, using the brand fonts.
2. **Leave text-safe space.** Keep the left 40 % of each frame visually quiet, with soft focus or plain surfaces, so the module title can overlay it (see the banner spec in `README.md`).
3. **Brand palette.** Steer every image towards deep forest green (#1B4332), warm gold (#D4AF37) accents, and warm white and natural materials.
4. **Accuracy beats beauty.** Teaching images of species, anatomy, disease signs and processing steps must be **real photographs** that a specialist has checked. AI images are for mood and hero banners only, and must not be captioned as specific species or conditions.
5. **Welfare and food safety.** Never generate images of live snails being boiled, salted or handled roughly, or of staff without gloves at processing stations.
6. **People:** show a range of ages, genders and ethnicities across the set, in line with the course's health-equity and smallholder audience.
7. **Midjourney parameters used below:** `--ar 16:9` (aspect ratio), `--style raw` (less stylised, more photographic), `--no` (exclusions). Add your preferred model version (`--v`) when you run them.

## Before Publishing an AI Image

- [ ] No garbled text, extra limbs, malformed shells or impossible objects
- [ ] Nothing contradicts course standards (gloves worn, no salt near live snails, clean surfaces, no live snails in processing images)
- [ ] Not captioned as a specific species, disease or procedure
- [ ] Left-side text-safe area works with the title overlay at both desktop and mobile crops
- [ ] Generator's commercial-use licence terms checked for your plan
- [ ] Alt text written (describe the scene, e.g. "Indoor snail farm with tiered racks and misting lines")
